Privacy Policy

Wolfeboro Branch sends occasional text messages about meetinghouse cleaning. This policy says what that program collects and how Wolfeboro Branch uses it.

Information we collect

  • Your name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • The date you agreed in person to receive these messages
  • Replies you send to those messages, including STOP

How we use it

Wolfeboro Branch uses this information to text you when it is your turn to clean the meetinghouse, to read a reply you send about that reminder, and to stop texting you if you opt out.

Messages are delivered by Twilio. Twilio receives your phone number only so it can deliver the message.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

Wolfeboro Branch keeps your name and number while you are on the cleaning reminder list. If you reply STOP, or if you ask to be removed, the number is kept only so later reminders are not sent to you.
